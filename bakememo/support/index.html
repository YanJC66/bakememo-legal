快速上手

  1. 首页选择“记一炉”空白起炉、“先看食谱”照现成配方做，或“迭代一炉”从已有炉记继续。
  2. 在记录纸上填写配方；烘焙百分比会实时计算，也可给任一步骤开始计时。
  3. 完成后点“入档”。从炉记查看血脉链，再用“再做一炉”继续迭代。

需要注册账号吗？

不需要。BakeMemo 没有 App 账号系统，打开即可使用。

没有 iCloud 能用吗？

可以，核心流程完整离线可用。已入档炉记可通过你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库在设备间同步；草稿、个人默认价和计时留在本机。

成本和卡路里准确吗？

它们始终是明确标注的预估。总卡路里按配方和内置营养参考计算；只有填写实际成品重量后才显示每 100g 成品预估。成本使用离线静态参考价或你保存的个人包装价；静态参考不是实时汇率、交易报价或当地零售价。

教练点评是 AI 吗？

不是。它是本地规则给出的烘焙复盘提示，会同时说明效果与取舍，不是医疗、营养或食品安全建议。

如何备份或换机？

可从设置导出 .bakememo 文件自行保存，并在新设备导入。使用同一 iCloud 账号时，已入档炉记也可通过你的私有数据库同步。

计时通知没有响？

请在系统设置中允许 BakeMemo 通知。通知不可用时，App 内计时仍会继续。

反馈与报错

请邮件联系 jiahuixu606@outlook.com，并尽量附上机型、iOS/iPadOS 版本和复现步骤。只有你在自己的邮件 App 中确认发送后，这些信息才会交给开发者；它们只用于回复与排障，你可邮件要求删除。

English

Getting started

  1. From Home, choose “Log a Bake” for a blank start, “Browse Recipes” to follow one, or “Iterate” to continue from an existing log.
  2. Fill in the recipe sheet. Baker’s percentages update live, and you can start a timer on any step.
  3. Tap “Archive” when finished. Open the log to see its lineage, then use “Bake again” to continue iterating.

Do I need an account?

No. BakeMemo has no app account system and works immediately.

Does it work without iCloud?

Yes, the core workflow works fully offline. Archived logs can sync across your devices through your own private iCloud database; drafts, personal default prices, and timers stay local.

Are cost and calorie figures accurate?

They are always clearly labelled estimates. Total calories use the formula and built-in nutrition references; per-100g output appears only after you enter actual baked weight. Cost uses an offline static reference or your saved package prices. Static references are not live exchange rates, transaction quotes, or local retail prices.

Is the coach AI?

No. It uses local baking rules and shows both the intended effect and trade-off. It is not medical, nutrition, or food-safety advice.

How do I back up or switch devices?

Export a .bakememo file from Settings and keep it wherever you choose, then import it on the new device. Archived logs can also sync through your private database when both devices use the same iCloud account.

Why did a timer notification not fire?

Allow BakeMemo notifications in system Settings. In-app timing continues even when notifications are unavailable.

Feedback and bug reports

Email jiahuixu606@outlook.com with your device model, iOS/iPadOS version, and steps to reproduce. These details reach the developer only after you confirm sending in your mail app; they are used only to reply and troubleshoot, and you may request deletion by email.

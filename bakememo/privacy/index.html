中文

你的烘焙记录属于你。它们保存在本机，并可选择同步到你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库。

我们收集什么

BakeMemo 没有开发者服务器，不接入任何分析、广告或第三方 SDK，也不会自动向开发者发送炉记、照片、原料价或使用行为。

只有当你主动选择“意见反馈”并在自己的邮件 App 中确认发送时，你的邮件服务商才会把发件地址、邮件正文、BakeMemo 版本和 iOS/iPadOS 版本发送给开发者。这些信息只用于回复和排查问题，不用于广告或画像。你可以通过同一邮箱要求删除相关邮件。

数据存在哪里

  • 本机：草稿、个人默认原料价和计时只存在你的设备上；炉记也会保存在本机。
  • 你自己的 iCloud（可选）：已入档炉记及其中由你选择的照片、评分和备注，可通过你的 Apple CloudKit 私有数据库在设备间同步。数据由你的 Apple 账户持有，开发者没有用于浏览用户私有炉记的后台。草稿、个人默认价和计时不进入 iCloud。
  • 导出文件：你可用 App 内导出功能生成本地 .bakememo 文件；是否分享、发给谁完全由你决定。

照片与通知

  • 照片：通过系统 PhotosPicker 选择。App 只接收你主动选择的图片，不读取整个相册；图片不会上传给开发者。
  • 通知：可见通知只用于你主动开始的烘焙计时，不用于营销或召回。CloudKit 可能使用 Apple 的静默远程通知同步变化；它不会向开发者发送炉记内容。

我们不做的事

不做跨 App 或网站追踪，不投放广告，不建立用户画像，不出售数据，也不把数据分享给第三方。

儿童

本 App 不面向儿童营销。未成年人使用时，应由监护人按当地规则判断是否适合。App 不会自动向开发者发送其炉记或使用行为。

你的控制权

  • 可将单条炉记移到回收站，并从回收站永久删除。
  • 永久删除已同步炉记时，App 会从你的 iCloud 私有数据库删除对应记录；你也可在系统 Apple 账户与 iCloud 设置中管理 BakeMemo 数据。
  • 卸载 App 会移除只存在本机的数据；导出的文件由你自行保管或删除。

政策变更与联系

政策更新时，我们会修改本页生效日期并发布新版本。如有隐私问题或希望删除反馈邮件，请联系 jiahuixu606@outlook.com。

English

Your baking records belong to you. They stay on your device and can optionally sync through your own private iCloud database.

What we collect

BakeMemo has no developer server and includes no analytics, ads, or third-party SDKs. It does not automatically send the developer your bake logs, photos, ingredient prices, or usage.

Only when you choose Feedback and confirm sending a message in your own mail app does your email provider deliver your sender address, message, BakeMemo version, and iOS/iPadOS version to the developer. We use them only to reply and troubleshoot, never for advertising or profiling. You may ask us to delete the related email.

Where your data lives

  • On your device: drafts, personal default ingredient prices, and timers stay only on your device. Bake logs are also stored locally.
  • Your own iCloud (optional): archived bake logs, including photos, ratings, and notes you choose to attach, can sync through your Apple CloudKit private database. The data is held by your Apple account; the developer has no backend for browsing users’ private bake logs. Drafts, personal default prices, and timers do not go to iCloud.
  • Exported files: you can create a local .bakememo file from the app. Whether and with whom you share it is entirely your choice.

Photos and notifications

  • Photos: selected through the system PhotosPicker. The app receives only the images you choose, does not read your whole photo library, and never uploads them to the developer.
  • Notifications: visible notifications are only for bake timers you start, never marketing or re-engagement. CloudKit may use Apple’s silent remote notifications to sync changes; they do not send bake-log content to the developer.

What we do not do

No cross-app or website tracking, ads, profiling, selling of data, or sharing of data with third parties.

Children

The app is not marketed to children. A guardian should decide whether it is suitable for a minor under local rules. The app does not automatically send a minor’s logs or usage to the developer.

Your controls

  • Move an individual log to Trash and permanently delete it there.
  • Permanently deleting a synced log removes the matching record from your private iCloud database. You can also manage BakeMemo data in Apple Account and iCloud settings.
  • Uninstalling removes data that exists only locally. Exported files are yours to keep or delete.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version here. For privacy questions or requests to delete feedback email, contact jiahuixu606@outlook.com.

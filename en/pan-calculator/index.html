Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is uploaded or saved.Default example: a 15 round pan to a 20 round pan. Use the same unit for both pans (all centimetres or all inches).

How it works

Multiplier = new pan base area ÷ original pan base area

  • Round pan area = 3.1416 × diameter × diameter ÷ 4
  • Rectangular pan area = length × width

From a 15 round to a 20 round: 20 × 20 ÷ (15 × 15) ≈ 1.78, so multiply every ingredient by 1.78.

What it assumes

This assumes the batter sits at the same depth in both pans. For a deeper pan or a taller bake, add more on top. Baking time usually changes with the pan too, and this tool can’t work that out: go by the doneness check in your recipe, not the clock alone.

What about eggs?

When the multiplier isn’t a whole number, weigh the eggs: beat them and measure out the grams you need.

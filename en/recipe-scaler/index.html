Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is uploaded or saved.Default example: a batch of 12 butter cookies scaled to 30, a factor of 2.5.

How it works

Factor = servings you want ÷ servings the recipe makes　or　target weight ÷ original weight

Every ingredient is multiplied by the same factor, so the ratios stay the same. When a target weight is filled in, it is used and the servings are ignored.

What to watch after scaling

  • Eggs: when the result isn’t a whole egg, beat them and weigh out the grams.
  • Pans and trays: a different amount usually needs a different pan; see the pan conversion calculator.
  • Time: this tool scales amounts only. Mixing, proofing and baking times may change with the batch size; go by the checks in your recipe.

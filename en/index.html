The hardest part of baking
is not knowing what actually changed.

A little more water? Thirty more minutes of proofing? BakeMemo keeps every change on record.

A baking notebook
that helps you review

From formula to method to the final rating, everything about a bake lives on one recipe sheet.

01 · Recipe sheet

One sheet,
bread to set desserts

Bread, cakes, cookies, pastry and set desserts share one recipe sheet. Enter grams and baker’s percentages are worked out live — hydration at a glance.

02 · Step by step

Bake by cues,
not just the clock

Each step shows what to check and what to avoid, and you can run a timer on any step. In-app timing continues even when notifications are unavailable.

03 · Lineage

See what changed,
bake to bake

Archive each bake and every version of a recipe links into one lineage. Compare recipe changes with your recorded ratings — without presenting correlation as certain cause.

A local rule-based coach suggests one clear next step and shows the trade-off.

04 · Pantry

Know what’s missing
before you start

  • Tap a recipe you want to make and anything missing goes on your shopping list
  • Logging a bake deducts what you used
  • Filter for recipes your pantry already covers

The pantry and shopping list are free.

05 · Import

Bring in recipes
from paper

Paste text or photograph a paper recipe. Text recognition runs on your device, and you check each line before it’s saved as your own recipe.

Import is free.

06 · Cost & nutrition

Know what each bake costs

Start from reference prices; enter a package price once and every bake follows what you actually pay. Cost and nutrition are always labelled estimates, and per-100g figures appear only after you enter the actual baked weight.

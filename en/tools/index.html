Baking tools

Free baking calculators

The six sums that come up most when you bake bread and pastry, one small tool each. Everything runs in your browser: nothing is uploaded or saved, and there is no sign-up.

  • Baker’s percentage calculatorEnter grams to get baker’s percentages, hydration and total hydration with starter, then scale to any dough weight.
  • Dough temperature calculatorEnter target dough, room, flour and friction temperatures to get the water temperature.
  • Starter feeding calculatorEnter how much starter you want and the ratio; get grams of old starter, flour and water.
  • Recipe scalerEnter each ingredient in grams and scale by servings or to a target total weight.
  • Pan conversion calculatorEnter two pan sizes to get the recipe multiplier by base area and convert ingredient amounts.
  • Recipe cost calculatorEnter amounts and pack prices to get ingredient cost per batch, per piece and per 100 g, plus a reference price from a target cost share. Runs in your browser.

BakeMemo’s recipe sheet works out baker’s percentages and hydration live and rescales to a target dough weight; every bake you log gets an estimated ingredient cost. 62 recipes and your first three bakes are free, no sign-up.

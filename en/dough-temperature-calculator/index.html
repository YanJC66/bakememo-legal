Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is uploaded or saved.The default is a straight dough: 25 × 3 − (22 + 22 + 6) = 25°C. Enter 24 for the levain and it becomes 25 × 4 − 74 = 26°C. Use °C throughout.

How it works

Water temp = target dough temp × factors − (room + flour + friction + preferment)

For a straight dough (no preferment) use 3 factors and leave the preferment out. With a levain or old dough, use 4 and include the preferment temperature. Many breads aim for roughly 24–26°C; follow your recipe.

What to enter for friction

Mixing warms the dough; how much depends on hand or machine and how long you mix. Measure it once: estimate with the formula, check the actual dough temperature after mixing, and use the gap to correct your friction value next time.

If the answer is very cold or very warm

If the result is very cold, use iced water. If it’s very warm, don’t use hot water — too much heat can harm the yeast.

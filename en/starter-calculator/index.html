How it works

One part = starter you want ÷ (the three ratio numbers added up)

1:5:5 is 11 parts. For 150 g of starter, one part is about 13.6 g: 13.6 g old starter, 68.2 g each of flour and water.

When the flour and water numbers match (1:1:1, 1:2:2, 1:5:5) you are feeding equal flour and water, which is a 100% hydration starter.

Choosing a ratio

1:1:1 has the least food and peaks fastest, good for using it the same day or in a cool kitchen. 1:2:2 is a medium pace for everyday feeding. 1:5:5 or higher is slow to peak with milder sourness, good for hot weather or overnight builds. Time to peak depends heavily on temperature: the same ratio can peak in a few hours in a warm kitchen and take much longer when it’s cool.

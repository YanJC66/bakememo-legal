How it works

Baker’s % = ingredient weight ÷ total flour × 100%

  • Total flour counts only rows marked “Flour”; several flours together make 100%. Flour inside the starter isn’t included here, matching how most formulas are written.
  • Hydration = total “Water” ÷ total flour.
  • Total hydration first splits the starter into flour and water by its hydration (flour in starter = starter ÷ (1 + hydration)), then works out (water + starter water) ÷ (flour + starter flour).
  • Scaling: factor = target weight ÷ current weight, applied to every ingredient so the ratios stay the same. Allow 2–3% extra for losses in mixing and shaping.

Milk, eggs and butter contain water too. Here they count as “Other” and are left out of hydration; convert them by their water content yourself if you need to.
